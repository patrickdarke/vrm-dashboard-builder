Install
  1. Unzip. You get a folder named dashboard.
  2. Upload the whole folder to your website with SFTP, next to your other site files (the same place your index.php or WordPress files live).
  3. Open https://your-site.com/dashboard/ and sign in.

Needs PHP 7.4+ with cURL — standard on DreamHost, Bluehost, SiteGround, Hostinger and most others. Keep config.php private: it holds your token.
